    Logistics: from the warehouse to the customer

    Logistics is the organized movement of goods from storage to the people who ordered them. It covers managing transport, handling orders and running the vehicles.

    The warehouse

    Where every trip starts and ends. It is the blue D in the city.

    The orders

    Pin 1 is the pickup and pin 2 is the delivery.

    The vehicles

    Limited, costly and always on the move. The white van is one of them.

    Routing: the right road and the right order

    Routing decides which road a vehicle takes and in what order it visits its customers, so deliveries are faster and cheaper.

    Which road?

    Between two stops there are many roads. The best one costs the least time and fuel, not just the fewest turns.

    Which order?

    With several customers, a smart visiting order can save more than a faster road. Choose the pickup and delivery points in the city and watch the van find its way.

    Intelligent routing

    Intelligent routing uses algorithms, artificial intelligence and traffic data to choose routes, and to change them when there is congestion or an accident.

    1. 1Collect data: the map, the traffic and where every vehicle is.
    2. 2Calculate: an algorithm finds the cheapest route and the best order of customers.
    3. 3React: when something changes, calculate again and send the vehicle another way.

    Traditional or intelligent?

    Traditional

    Fixed routes, planned once, or decisions made by hand. When a road is blocked the driver waits or improvises.

    Intelligent

    Adapts to changing conditions and calculates again when it has to, so the vehicle keeps moving.

    In the city above, the traditional route is planned once on clear roads and never changes. The intelligent route is calculated again every time the roads change.

    What computer science adds

    Algorithms

    They calculate the routes. The city becomes a graph of junctions and roads, and the algorithm finds the cheapest path through it.

    Data analysis

    Traffic records show where and when roads clog. Traffic data helps the system understand where and when roads become congested.

    GPS

    Tells the system where every vehicle is right now, so plans can follow reality.

    Artificial intelligence

    Learns patterns, expects congestion before it happens and improves decisions over time.

    In this demo the algorithm is real, but the traffic you draw is simulated.

    LOGISTICS & SUPPLY CHAIN

    Logistics problems and solutions

    Five common challenges. One practical solution at a time.

    TRANSPORTATION

    High transport costs

    Reduce the cost of moving goods without slowing down deliveries.

    • Use route optimization to choose shorter and faster roads.
    • Combine orders in one truck to reduce the number of trips.
    • Monitor fuel use and maintain vehicles regularly.
    DELIVERY OPERATIONS

    Delivery delays

    Keep shipments moving and deliver orders on time.

    • Track shipments in real time to identify delays early.
    • Organize delivery schedules around traffic and delivery priorities.
    • Prepare alternative routes for traffic, accidents, or bad weather.
    WAREHOUSE MANAGEMENT

    Poor inventory management

    Keep the right products in stock and avoid shortages or excess inventory.

    • Use an inventory management system to monitor stock levels.
    • Use barcode scanning to update product quantities accurately.
    • Forecast customer demand to plan the right stock levels.
    SUPPLY CHAIN VISIBILITY

    Low visibility

    Give every logistics team a clear view of where goods are and when they will arrive.

    • Connect suppliers, warehouses, and delivery teams through one shared system.
    • Share real-time shipment location and status updates.
    • Keep information updated so teams can respond quickly to problems.
    CARGO SAFETY

    Damaged or lost goods

    Protect products throughout storage, handling, and transportation.

    • Use suitable packaging to protect goods during transportation.
    • Train workers in safe loading, unloading, and storage.
    • Track each shipment with barcode or RFID technology.
